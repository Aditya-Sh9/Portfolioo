<CaseSection index="01" title="Overview">

Lexium is a full-stack legal services marketplace designed to connect citizens with verified legal professionals such as advocates, mediators, arbitrators, notaries, document writers, and tax consultants.

The platform supports the complete journey from discovering a professional and booking a consultation to filing a case, tracking its progress, completing the service, leaving a review, and releasing the associated payment.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection index="02" title="Three roles">

The platform is built around three distinct roles: citizens, legal professionals, and administrators.

- **Citizens** can browse approved professionals, view their profiles and services, book consultations, file and track cases, and submit reviews.
- **Providers** have their own dashboard for managing incoming cases, appointments, active cases, profiles, reviews, and earnings.
- **Administrators** supervise the platform by approving providers, managing users, monitoring cases and transactions, and controlling the release of escrowed payments.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection index="03" title="Case lifecycle and escrow">

A major part of Lexium is its case lifecycle and escrow system. When a citizen files a case, it moves through defined stages such as pending, under review, in progress, awaiting documents, resolved, and closed, with timeline entries recording important updates.

Once a consultation is completed, the associated payment enters escrow and can only be released by an administrator after the linked case reaches an eligible resolved or closed state.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection index="04" title="Compliance and performance">

- **Providers** get performance badges and leaderboard ranking, compliance notices and guidance, and a transparent earnings ledger alongside their dashboard analytics.
- **Administrators** handle complaint moderation and resolution, and can escalate issues with warnings, earnings deductions, or escrow holds. They also have leaderboard and performance analytics, and audit trails for all compliance actions.

</CaseSection>
